# Investigation playbook

You own the answer. Investigation is read-only: change no code, config, or external state.

## Steps

1. **Shape the answer to the question.** A choice between alternatives: compare them in a table of tradeoffs.
2. **Answer from evidence** (`principle-label-claims`).
3. **Stop at the answer.** If it shows that a change is needed, propose the change. It runs under the Bug fix, Feature, or Refactoring playbook once the user asks for it.

## Reply

Every reply includes these sections:

- **Answer**: the answer first. For "are we sure?" questions, your real judgment with reasons, pushing back if the premise is wrong.
- **Evidence**: each supporting fact with its file, commit, or source.
